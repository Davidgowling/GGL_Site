How we run the group

We are owner-managed, so decisions are made quickly and for the long term rather than the next quarter.

Brands with their own identity
Our businesses serve different customers, so each keeps its own name, team and way of trading.
Shared operations
Warehousing, logistics, systems and finance are run centrally, so every business gets the benefit of the group's scale.
Long relationships
We work with the same manufacturers, retailers and fitters year after year, and we look after those relationships.
Investment for the long term
We reinvest in stock, people and new brands, and we are open to acquiring businesses that fit the group.
